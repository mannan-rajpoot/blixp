What Blixp does

Add a screen recording, style it, and export a video that’s ready to post.

  • Clean screen presentation

    Your recording sits in a rounded screen that matches the video’s own shape. Adjust its size and corner roundness, and switch the soft shadow on or off.

  • Custom backgrounds

    Choose from seven preset backgrounds, from plain white to soft gradients, or pick any color of your own.

  • Title and profile

    Add an optional title above the screen, plus a profile photo and name or handle in the bottom left corner.

  • Exports for social

    Export a Reel (9:16), a Post (4:5) or a Square video at 1080 px wide, as MP4 at 30 or 60 fps, up to 30 seconds. Sound is included when your browser supports it.

  • Live preview

    Play and scrub through your video in the editor to check every detail before you export.

  • Stays on your device

    Editing and export happen in your browser, so your recording never leaves your device.
